# Product film assets

The default landing page does not load these files. They are retained for the optional
offline film renderer at `/film-render` and for provenance of earlier visual regression
fixtures.

- `school-film.webm`: 20-second, 24 fps product demonstration rendered with Three.js and encoded offline with WebCodecs. The screen uses illustrative task data, not a recording of a user's account.
- `school-film-poster.jpg`: First frame of the film.
- `study-background.png`: Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, then copied into this project. It is an original photographic-style backdrop.

## Background generation prompt

Create a photorealistic cinematic background plate for an AI school productivity app product film. Ultra-wide 16:9 landscape. A quiet modern Korean school library study desk beside a tall window, shot on a cinema camera 50mm lens with shallow depth of field, beautiful late afternoon sunlight. Warm rich walnut tabletop fills bottom half, soft out-of-focus leafy trees outside at upper right, dark blurred bookshelves on the left. A subtly blurred open notebook and graphite pencil at far left foreground, off-white coffee cup at very far right. Central and center-right tabletop MUST be completely empty, reserved for compositing a large upright smartphone later. Camera at about 25 degrees above tabletop looking slightly downward. Premium real advertising photography, rich natural textures, soft long window shadows, gentle halation, muted warm olive and cool sage color grading, realistic restrained exposure. Left 40 percent deliberately darker, quiet and uncluttered for overlay white headline. No people, no phone, no computer, no text, no logos, no UI, no geometric abstraction, no illustration, no 3D render appearance. Output one full bleed high resolution landscape photograph.
